### Community Guidelines

1. **Be respectful** - Treat everyone with kindness
2. **Be constructive** - Focus on solutions, not problems
3. **Be patient** - Maintainers are volunteers
4. **Be helpful** - Answer questions when you can

### Bug Reports

Report bugs via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/your-org/yolo-toys/issues):

```markdown
**Description**
A clear description of the bug.

**To Reproduce**
Steps to reproduce:
1. Run `docker run ...`
2. Send request to `/infer`
3. See error

**Expected**
What you expected to happen.

**Environment**
- OS: Ubuntu 22.04
- Docker: 24.0
- GPU: NVIDIA T4
```

## Code of Conduct

### Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

### Our Standards

**Positive behavior:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Trolling, insulting comments, and personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

### Enforcement

Violations should be reported to the maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
